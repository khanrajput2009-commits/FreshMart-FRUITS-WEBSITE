Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying fruits online in Karachi with FreshMart.

Do you deliver fresh fruits all over Karachi?

Yes. We deliver across DHA, Clifton, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Gulistan-e-Johar, North Nazimabad, PECHS and most other areas of Karachi. Choose your area at checkout, or message us on WhatsApp if you are unsure.

How quickly can I get my fruit?

Order before 6 pm for same-day online fruit delivery in Karachi. Orders placed later are delivered the next morning.

What if my fruit is not fresh?

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery and we will replace the fruit or refund you. Freshness is guaranteed on every order.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery is free on orders over PKR 3,000. Smaller orders have a flat PKR 250 delivery fee.

Can I order fruit baskets or bulk fruit for events?

Yes. We prepare fruit hampers and bulk orders for weddings, offices and iftar gatherings. Contact us on WhatsApp or through the contact page and we will build a basket to your budget.
