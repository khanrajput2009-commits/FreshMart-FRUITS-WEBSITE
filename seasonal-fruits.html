Winter Harvest: Citrus, Pomegranate & Berries

Cool Karachi mornings bring the sweetest kinnow, juicy Valencia oranges, ruby pomegranates and fragrant guavas. These fresh seasonal fruits are packed with vitamin C, perfect for chai-time snacking, fresh juice and fruit chaat.

  • Naturally rich in vitamin C and antioxidants
  • Best eaten fresh, juiced or in fruit chaat
  • Freshest from November to February

Summer Harvest: Mangoes, Melons & Lychee

When the heat arrives, so do Sindh's golden mangoes, cooling watermelon and garma, juicy peaches and rose-scented lychee. Order online for fruit that is cold, ripe and ready to enjoy.

  • Ideal for milkshakes, kulfi and cold desserts
  • Mango season peaks from May to August
