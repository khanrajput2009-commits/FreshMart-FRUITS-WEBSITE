About FreshMart, the Best Fruit Shop in Karachi

We started with one simple belief: Karachi deserves fruit that tastes the way it should. Today we are an online fruit shop bringing fresh fruits and fresh seasonal fruits to thousands of doorsteps.

Our story

From a Family Fruit Stall to Online Fruit Delivery in Karachi

FreshMart began as a small family fruit stall, built on early mornings at the wholesale market and a reputation for honest, ripe fruit. Customers kept asking the same question: can you deliver?

So we built the answer: an online fruit shop in Karachi that keeps the care of a neighbourhood stall and adds the convenience of ordering from your phone. We still choose fruit by hand, we still taste what we sell, and we still treat every order like it is going to our own family.

Freshness & quality commitment

Our Path From Orchard to Your Doorstep

Four simple steps keep our fresh fruits in Karachi tasting the way nature intended.

  1. 1

    Sourced from growers

    We work with trusted orchards and wholesale markets to choose fruit at the right stage of ripeness.

  2. 2

    Hand-inspected

    Every crate is checked by our buyers; bruised, underripe or overripe fruit never makes the cut.

  3. 3

    Cooled & packed

    Fruit is washed, graded and packed in breathable, cooled crates to lock in flavour.

  4. 4

    Delivered fast

    Our riders bring your order to your door within your chosen time slot, the same day.
